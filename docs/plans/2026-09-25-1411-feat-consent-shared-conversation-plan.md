# Consent for a duo in a shared conversation - Plan (#192)

## Goal Capsule

- **Objective:** A duo is reachable only where its owner allows: an invite is held and surfaced (never auto-joined), joining is the owner's explicit allow recorded owner-side, answering in a shared room is gated on that record structurally (before any retrieval/generation), what the duo says there stays scoped by the asker exactly as `answerPublicly` scopes it, and withdrawal stops answering at once and leaves the room.
- **Means:** a new consent record `duo/room-consent.json` (`src/workspace/consent.ts`), a consent-aware question filter (`answerableQuestionFrom` in `questions.ts`, additive), invite facts in the message contract (`invitesFromSync` + `RoomInvite`), three seam operations (`onInvite`/`joinRoom`/`leaveRoom`), service-level hold/allow/refuse/withdraw + a send-time re-check and leave reconciliation, and owner routes on the daemon (`/workspace/invites`, `/workspace/rooms`).
- **Authority hierarchy:** issue #192's four ACs; `docs/workspace-platform.md` §6 ("It joins rooms by invitation, the same as a person"; credentials never leave the daemon) and §5 (the boundary); #219's successor notes (the duo-to-duo loop guard); duo#65 (mentions build on this) and duo#67 (asker-facing provenance builds on this).
- **Stop conditions:** every AC mapped to tests; full suite + typecheck green under pinned Node 22.22.3; the workspace egress/import/provisioning scans stay green and unweakened; shared-file edits strictly additive (sibling #190 chain).
- **Territory:** consent is primary — `src/workspace/consent.ts` (new), the invite/withdraw service surface, the routes. `service.ts`/`questions.ts`/`messages.ts`/`transport.ts` get additive members only (one receive-line swap to the wrapping filter). Out of scope: files/grounding (#190), E2EE rooms (off in the pilot, org-read §2), bridge consent (platform record §8), mention syntax (duo#65).

## Key Decisions

- **The consent record is a checked-in `duo/` JSON file, not an encrypted-store table.** Consent is owner policy in exactly the class of `duo/directory.json` (who may ask) and `duo/muted-channels.json` (where the duo speaks): it must be reviewable by hand ("which rooms is my duo reachable in?" — open one file, see every room with its provenance) and reversible by hand. Nothing in it is a secret — room ids, names, inviter ids are homeserver-visible facts; the store's encryption exists for the corpus. Per-decision reads (the channel-mutes precedent) give withdrawal immediacy with no cache to invalidate. The store is where *revocable secrets* live (device/ask tokens) and where state sharing the corpus's lifecycle lives (read events); consent is neither. A store table would also push the logic across the pinned boundary scan (src/workspace cannot import store modules), splitting one concept across two directories.
- **One record, keyed by room, status-valued; executed exits are deleted.** `duo/room-consent.json` is a sorted array of `{ roomId, status: allowed|refused|withdrawn, name, inviterId, joinedMemberCount, decidedAt }`. `refused`/`withdrawn` entries exist only while their mechanical consequence (leaving the room) is still pending — once the leave lands, the entry is deleted. That deletion is the design: a lingering refusal record would tempt future code to auto-refuse a re-invite, which is exactly the loop the issue forbids ("a re-invite is a fresh decision"). The fresh-decision property is structural instead: the invite is gone server-side (the leave rejected it), so only a *new* invite act can re-open the room, and every new invite is held and surfaced again.
- **Joining is the consented act, and join precedes record.** `allowInvite` = `joinRoom` over the seam, then the record write. A crash between the two leaves the room joined-but-unrecorded, which the gate answers with silence (the safe direction); the reverse order would leave a consented-but-unjoined record the daemon can never act on (nothing auto-joins — "re-join without a fresh consent act" is refused in both directions). A join failure records nothing and leaves the invite pending server-side, so the owner's retry is a real retry.
- **The gate is the question filter, ahead of the pipeline.** `answerableQuestionFrom` wraps `directQuestionFrom`: direct two-member rooms stay the v1 default posture (no record needed — #186's shipped behavior, unchanged); any other room yields a question only when the record says `allowed`. An explicit non-allow decision (`refused`/`withdrawn`) vetoes the default posture too — the owner's latest word about a room is stronger than the posture. The check runs in `receive()`, before dedupe marks, before the answer chain, before any store open — the same short-circuit shape as the refusal gate. A malformed file blocks shared rooms (fail closed on the new surface) and leaves the direct posture intact (fail stable on the old one), logged owner-side.
- **The duo-to-duo loop guard stays upstream of consent.** The sender-kind guard (#221: another duo's message is never a question) runs before the consent lookup, so no consent state can create an answering loop — pinned by test in room context. No allow-time heuristic (the invite carries counts, not member ids; and a duo-invited room may legitimately hold people); the structural guard is the enforcement.
- **Withdrawal is immediate, twice-checked.** `withdrawRoom` writes `withdrawn` (the gate stops at the next read — including the direct-posture veto), then leaves over the seam; a question whose draft is already in flight is re-checked immediately before its send and dropped (logged, event id forgotten so a replay re-decides under current consent — the send-failure stance). A failed leave leaves the `withdrawn` entry as the memory of an unfinished exit; each successful connect retries the leave and deletes the entry once it lands.
- **Scope follows the asker; the room never enters the resolution.** The answering closure in `commands/serve.ts` derives the asker from `question.senderId` alone (`askerContextForSender` — its signature has no room parameter), and `answerPublicly` applies the uniform public ceiling. Being in a consented room grants no lenience to anyone in it — pinned end-to-end: identical outcomes and identical logged asker refs for the same sender across 1:1 and shared room, and the same conservative decline for an unresolved sender from any room.
- **The invite shape is the message contract's, used by the seam.** `RoomInvite { roomId, inviterId, name, joinedMemberCount }` is defined in `messages.ts` beside `invitesFromSync` (closing the "invite/left rooms are #192" gap the contract's v1 scope note names) and imported by the transport seam for `onInvite` — one definition, both sides. `joinedMemberCount` is what the invite's sync slice carries (Matrix stripped state is heroes-limited) — stated, not inflated.
- **The owner surface is the daemon's HTTP routes** (the same surface drafts/review/mutes use): `GET /workspace/invites` (held invites with name, inviter, member count), `POST /workspace/invites/:roomId/allow|refuse`, `GET /workspace/rooms` (the record), `POST /workspace/rooms/:roomId/withdraw`. Device-token auth applies as everywhere else — ask links cannot consent anything. Arrival is also logged owner-side (console.error, the no-request error surface). The client renders these (duo#65); no CLI in this PR.

## Requirements (AC → tests)

- **R1 only reachable where allowed:** `answerableQuestionFrom` — no record → group-room message is not a question; `allowed` → it is; `refused`/`withdrawn` → not, including as a veto over the direct posture; `undefined` lookup → byte-for-byte the pre-#192 filter.
- **R2 joining is consented:** the service holds a delivered invite (surfaced with facts, not auto-joined, nothing answered in the room pre-allow); allow joins then records and the room's questions flow; a join failure records nothing and keeps the invite pending; allow/refuse with no held invite is a typed 404; refusing leaves (rejecting the invite) and deletes the executed entry; a re-invite re-surfaces as a fresh decision.
- **R3 scope follows the asker:** same sender, 1:1 vs consented shared room → identical asker context and identical asker-visible outcome; an unresolved sender gets the conservative outcome from any room; a resolved high-clearance sender gets no lenience from resolution or room (public ceiling) — asserted on real `answerPublicly` outcomes, logged refs, and wire bodies via `runServe`.
- **R4 withdrawal immediate:** withdraw stops answering in that room at once, including a mid-draft answer (re-checked before send, never sent, logged); the leave goes over the seam; a failed leave retries on reconnect and the entry is deleted once executed; `withdrawRoom` on an unrecorded room is an honest typed 404.
- **R5 loop guard holds in room context:** a duo-authored message in a consented room is never a question (pinned at the filter and through the service).
- **R6 contract + adapter:** `invitesFromSync` parses the invite section (inviter = the invite membership event's sender, name from `m.room.name`, joined count from join-state member events, nulls when absent); the Matrix adapter normalizes `Room.myMembership` invite changes into seam invites and implements `joinRoom`/`leaveRoom` (stub-pinned).
- **R7 routes:** the five `/workspace/*` routes over a real daemon — auth-gated like every non-`/health` route, 404s typed, and the whole flow end-to-end (invite → allow → answer in room → withdraw → silence).
